= How to transition 7-Mode SMB Storage to ONTAP
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[lead]
This section covers the step-by-step procedure for transitioning a source 7-Mode SMB share to an ONTAP system.

NOTE: NetApp assumes that the 7-Mode and ONTAP systems are SMB licensed. The destination SVM is created. The source and destination SMB shares are exported. XCP is installed and licensed.
